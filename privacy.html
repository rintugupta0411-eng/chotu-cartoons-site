Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Chhotu Cartoons ("we") runs a small private publishing tool that uploads videos we create to our own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

What we access

  • YouTube (Google): with our own permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload only to upload videos and thumbnails to our channel.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): the tool uses a Page access token to publish videos and Reels to our own Page and linked Instagram account.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell personal data of viewers or other users.
  • We do not access anyone else's accounts.
  • We do not share data with third parties, except sending our own videos to YouTube and Meta to publish them.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and are used only to publish our content. We keep a simple log of uploaded video IDs.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Viewers

When you watch our videos on YouTube, Facebook or Instagram, those platforms' own privacy policies apply. We do not receive personal data about you from the tool.

Children

Our stories are for families. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
